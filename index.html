GAME RULES

Give every number
a little space.

Drag each number from 1 to 8 into an empty box. Use every number exactly once. The game checks your arrangement as soon as all eight numbers are placed, and each completed arrangement counts as an attempt.

Consecutive numbers must not touch horizontally or vertically; diagonal neighbours are allowed. Numbers cannot be moved once placed. If the puzzle has a conflict, select Clear puzzle to try again. Your attempt count is saved in this browser.
